Shot Clock Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy explains what data Shot Clock: Hoop Puzzle (also published as “24 Saniye: Basketbol Bulmaca”, the “App”) processes and how. Data controller: Çağatay Ölmez (contact: cagatayolmez99@gmail.com).

What we don't collect

The App does not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or advertising identifier (IDFA). There are no accounts and no analytics tools. The App does not track you across other companies' apps and websites, so it does not ask for tracking permission (ATT).

Game progress

Your stars, unlocked hints and language choice are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. You can delete them with Settings → Reset progress.

Ads

The App shows two kinds of ads: a rewarded ad you choose to watch (to unlock a hint) and an occasional interstitial ad between levels. Ads are served by Google AdMob as non-personalized ads. AdMob may process device and connection information (such as IP address, device model and OS version) to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Details: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases

The “Remove Ads” purchase is made through Apple's App Store. Your payment details are never shared with us; your purchase is verified through Apple's system. Once purchased, no ads are shown.

Your rights

To exercise your data protection rights (access, correction, deletion, etc.), contact us at the email above. We do not hold any data that can be linked to you.

Children

The App is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

When this policy changes, this page is updated.
