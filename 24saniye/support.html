FAQ

How do I play?
Drag a player and drop: drop the ball handler on a teammate to pass, on the hoop to shoot, on an empty square to dribble. Drop an off-ball player on a defender to set a screen. After every move the defense reacts by its own simple rules. “How to Play” in the main menu lists all the rules.

I'm stuck on a level.
The HINT button in the game shows the coach's note and the first moves of a 3-star solution. Every level has at least one 3-star solution.

I bought “Remove Ads” but still see ads.
Go to Settings → “Restore purchases”. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID.

“Couldn't load an ad right now.”
Check your internet connection and try again in a moment.

My progress is gone.
Your stars are stored only on your device; deleting the app deletes your progress. Your purchase comes back with “Restore purchases”.

How do I change the language?
Settings → Language: choose Türkçe or English.
